A make.fun entry for **Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second**:
one sentence, 200 characters at most, pitching a bizarre startup idea that
sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell.

## The pitch

> We train crows to pick up litter in city parks, pay them in peanuts, and bill the council per kilo — the cheapest street cleaners a city ever hired.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, deterministic) writes the pitch to
`/output/entry.txt`. It validates the text against the bounty gate first:
1–200 characters, UTF-8, exactly one sentence, not only whitespace.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
